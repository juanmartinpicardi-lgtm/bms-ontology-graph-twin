What is real, what is not

  • Real (this project's knowledge graph): equipment, points, classes, method, confidence, review status, source rows, every feeds / hasPart relationship. Rejected items are left out; reviews show up on reload.
  • Inferred: which floor each asset is on. The source has no floor or space tags; AHUs in name order take Level 1–4 and their zones follow them, plant goes in the basement, AHUs, fans and towers on the roof.
  • Generated: rooms, furniture, facade, site, equipment models. Parametric per type, at real-world sizes, not the actual building.
  • Simulated: temperatures, alarms, fan speeds, point values. On Alpha, VAV 1-2-81 and CH-2 run warm on purpose, to match the agent tour. Units are inferred from each point's class.
  • Analysis is for reading data; Realistic is the building as it would look, with time of day.
